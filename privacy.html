Privacy Policy — Nexus Legal Copilot POC

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes the current proof-of-concept version. It must be updated before connecting the Nexus AI service or collecting or sending user content elsewhere.

What this prototype does

The current prototype displays questions typed by a tester in that tester's browser session and shows a placeholder response. It does not connect to the Nexus AI backend, automatically read Meet transcripts, record meeting audio, or send chat text to another service. The page does not save chat text to browser storage or a developer database.

Meet activity

When a user starts an activity, the prototype uses the Google Meet Add-ons SDK to request Meet's activity notification and join flow. Google processes information needed to operate Meet and the add-on platform under Google's own terms and privacy disclosures.

Google account information

The current web page does not read or store a tester's Google profile or email address. The Google Cloud Marketplace configuration may display basic email/profile permissions during installation. Those permissions should be removed if they are not required before broader release.

Retention and choices

Chat text is held in the open page only and is lost when that page is closed or reloaded. A tester can ask to leave the draft test using the draft tester opt-out page. The developer will remove the requested address from the draft tester list.

Changes and contact

This policy must be revised before any backend, AI provider, analytics, account identity, or persistence feature is added. For privacy questions, contact harishtholeti.greenaiservices@gmail.com.
